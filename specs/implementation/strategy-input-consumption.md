# Strategy Account and Market Subscription Consumption Gate

`HostConfig.projection_inputs` (`tickwire-app::hosted::inputs`) verifies that a running strategy worker actually consumed the complete account report stream, venue catalog, and external market topic approved by `strategy.account_ids` and `strategy.topic_ids` in its permission-scoped configuration projection. Rather than trusting launch templates alone, `App` validates static deployment bindings before spawning any child process and then withholds the worker's first outgoing trading intent until a live checkpoint query (`FreshObservation::inputs()`) reconstructs and confirms the SDK's saved `AccountInput`, `MarketInput`, `ObservedJoin`, and `ObservedReportInput` views at the current merged cut.

The supported launch profile binds exactly one account report lane and one external normalized market topic per registered worker. If `projection_launch` or `projection_parameters` are also enabled, every active binding must name the identical `ProjectionObject` and `Scope`. `InputsProof` attests only to actual subscription consumption at the observed checkpoint; economic catalog validation (`instrument-catalog.md`), `allowed_symbols` filtering, and account risk-budget enforcement remain owned by their respective components.

## State Model and Bounds

`inputs::Config` uses strict `#[serde(deny_unknown_fields)]` JSON/TOML parsing to bind `object_file`, `profile::Binding`, `profile::Scope`, `AccountBinding`, `MarketBinding`, and `timeout_ns` (`<= 30` seconds). `Prepared::prepare` validates every mapping before `App` creates runtime directories or spawns children.

| Approved Domain | Pre-Launch Static Checks (`prepare`) | Post-Launch Checkpoint Checks (`observe` & `extract`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account & report lane | `strategy.account_ids`, `account_views`, and `AccountBinding` name the exact single account (`venue_views` names its single Binance venue); matches canonical `CanonicalBaseline` bytes/digest, `Scope`, account and input physical journals (`risk_domain_id`), `CommandBinding`, execution route, `report_owner` (`role == 4`), and 512-byte report (`kind 3`) / 1,024-byte intent (`kind 2`) bank headers. | `ObservedInputs` contains exactly 1 `ObservedAccountInput` matching `CanonicalBaseline` digest/length/scope/`account_epoch`, `position == ready.exec_report_sequence == cursor.sequence`, and `input_digest == observed.account_input_digest()`, plus `ObservedReportInput` matching `ReportWriter` header, `SubscriptionKey`, `ReportIdentity`, and `ready.report_ring_sequence == first_seq + position`. |
| Market topic & `JOIN` | `strategy.topic_ids`, `ipc.topics`, and `MarketBinding` name the single `NormalizedMarket` (`StrictTrading`, `qos == 1`, `event_class == 7`, `kind 1`) topic; matches external `ConnectConfig`, canonicalized bank header (normalizing only a zero-hash template), `producer` (`role == 2`), nonzero `catalog_hash`, `instrument_set_version`, and sorted `(instrument_id, instrument_version, required_states)` members. | `ObservedInputs` contains exactly 1 `ObservedMarketInput` with a present `ObservedJoin` (extracted from recovery dependency kind `11` via a verified `TWSC0001` -> `TWSB0001` `JOIN` and sealed snapshot) whose bank `ResourceHeader`, `catalog_hash`, `instrument_set_version`, joined `(id, version, states)`, and current `(id, version)` match `MarketBinding`. |
| Product, environment & catalog | Maps `"Spot"` to `Product::Spot` and `"Usdm"` to `LinearPerpetual` or `LinearFuture`; maps `"simulated"` to `Environment::Test` and `"production"` to `Environment::Production`. Because `NormalizedMarket` headers carry zero venue/product discriminators, every market instrument must exist in the account `CanonicalBaseline` catalog with matching `product`, `instrument_version`, `catalog_hash`, and `catalog_version`. Optional runtime/bootstrap `Catalog` bytes must match `venue_views.instrument_catalog_ref`. | Resolves optional dependency kind `9` (`Catalog`) against `checkpoint.source`, validates checkpoint catalog economics, confirms `joined.catalog_hash == catalog.catalog_hash()`, and verifies `inputs.catalog()` raw bytes match the prepared catalog `Object`. |

## Core Invariants

* **Reconstruction without user-code execution**: Inside `tickwire-strategy-host`, `tickwire_strategy_sdk::replay::application::observed::inspect(&Checkpoint)` reconstructs the worker's saved stream registry, encoded account/market views, bank-to-stream bindings, account input history, and runtime cursors directly from durable checkpoint bytes without invoking strategy user callbacks. `tickwire_release::account_inputs::verify` cross-checks the `HandoverSeal`, raw checkpoint, encoded inputs, and account input stream before exposing `ObservedInputs`.
* **Honest absence vs. corruption**: A structurally valid checkpoint with zero accounts (not yet attached) or multiple accounts (unsupported by a single-report `Host`) yields `inputs = None`, and a valid post-transition market bank lacking original `TWSC0001` -> `TWSB0001` `JOIN` snapshot evidence in dependency kind `11` yields `join = None`—never backfilling missing facts from a launch template. Any malformed, truncated, or mismatched seal/dependency payload fails immediately with `Error::Invalid`. When `projection_inputs` is enabled, `App` requires `Some(ObservedInputs)` and `Some(ObservedJoin)` with complete matching facts.
* **Provenance boundaries**: In `ObservedInputs`, the report bank header and `ReportIdentity` come from the host's authoritative `Reports` writer, subscription watermarks come from the verified shared-memory report cursor, and the market-data producer identity comes from authenticated startup registration (observing the external market-data publisher's live kernel `/proc` state would require its own supervisor observation).

## Operations and Failure Semantics

* **Unified first-intent gate with `projection_parameters`**: While the internal observation query is in flight, `App` continues driving market and account report inputs into the worker so it can reach the required watermark floor, but holds every outbound strategy intent in queue. When both `projection_parameters` and `projection_inputs` are configured, they share a single fresh `ObservationRequest` challenge, kernel process double-sample, and merged `Checkpoint` (`configuration.checkpoint() == inputs.checkpoint()`, `cut`, and `source`), bounded by the *earlier* of the two configured deadlines.
* **Rate limiting, timeout, and recovery re-verification**: The internal gate advances at most once every `10 ms`, resamples monotonic time after disk and `/proc` reads before publishing `InputsProof` (and `ParametersProof`), and terminates and reaps the managed child if the deadline expires or any field mismatches. Restarting or recovering a launch clears prior runtime proofs and runs a fresh observation before admitting new intents.
