# Scalar execution journal and recovery runtime

`BusinessJournal` persists the scalar execution engine's prepared intents, fills, fees, terminal outcomes and pre-send aborts. Its schema-2 business records are separate from the multi-asset account journal. The bootstrap binds codec version, physical configuration, venue, account, product and initial capital. The caller supplies independently trusted bootstrap expectations. [Journal](../../crates/tickwire-business-journal/src/lib.rs) · [Business codec](journal-codec.md) · [Account journal](account-journal.md)

One business event occupies one physical data record. Business position matches physical sequence. The journal verifies domain, journal epoch and writer epoch against the physical envelope. It recomputes the logical chain from genesis. A private `WalAppendReceipt` only becomes available after the physical batch is synchronized. It proves coverage by a local physical prefix. Trading permission and replication require additional evidence. [Business journal](../../crates/tickwire-business-journal/src/lib.rs)

## Identity, admission and send

A new `PreparedIntent` must name the current writer as its originating execution epoch. An exact durable-record retry returns the original business position and logical hash, including after writer takeover. Its covering physical frontier may have advanced. A conflicting binding for the same intent ID or intent sequence poisons the journal. Later facts retain the original order's intent identity and epoch while the envelope names the current writer. Mismatched order identity, an incompatible fact or arithmetic failure freezes the runtime. Received economic facts are persisted before the reducer applies them. [Runtime](../../crates/tickwire-business-journal/src/runtime.rs)

The runtime reads a fresh gate before reservation. A trusted `CommitAuthorizer` supplies a verified closure bound to the whole command. After `PreparedIntent` sync, the runtime re-reads the gate and checks strategy and writer generations, configuration, deadline, lease and validity. `begin_send` records the possibility of output before invoking the exchange interface. Uncertain output keeps the reservation and freezes further risk. Only the current trusted exchange call can report `NoBytesAccepted`. The runtime first persists `AbortedBeforeSend`, then releases through the reducer. External callers cannot submit that local proof through `record_fact`. This runtime implements `LocalDurable`. It refuses a requested replica policy it cannot satisfy. [Submit and abort](../../crates/tickwire-business-journal/src/runtime.rs)

## Replay

The runtime builds a new frozen engine, verifies each admission's existing recovery closure and replays the unified stream in order. Recovery cannot publish missing objects, contact the exchange or enable a new sender. Even an empty journal recovers frozen. A missing closure, invalid fact, mismatched identity or failed arithmetic prevents publication of any candidate runtime. The physical journal may trim an unsealed final batch. Detecting deletion of an entire previously acknowledged suffix requires an independent trusted frontier. Historical file tests exercise sync errors, partial abort writes, subprocess termination and directory changes. [Runtime tests](../../crates/tickwire-business-journal/tests/runtime.rs)
