# USDⓈ-M income and account observations

`CanonicalAccountInput` carries normalized income and private account observations into the account reducer. Order `CanonicalEvent` retains its own meaning. A canonical input is limited to 60 KiB, with at most 64 assets and 128 positions per observation. The input WAL preserves authenticated raw bytes and the canonical decision. The owner syncs this input first, validates a candidate ledger, syncs its account record and only then publishes the new state. [Canonical inputs](../../crates/tickwire-venue/src/account.rs)

## Income and the opening baseline

Income is unique per account and `(incomeType, tranId)`. Asset version, wallet, symbol, trade ID, amount, time and info belong to its exact body. A reused key with different contents conflicts. An approved baseline has an explicit covered set. Replaying a covered row leaves cash unchanged. A previously unseen key dated before the cutoff remains unresolved.

* Post-baseline `FUNDING_FEE` books the actual signed cash flow. A debit reduces capital. An unattributed credit increases the wallet with an equal hold on available capital. New funding stays frozen pending reconciliation.
* `REALIZED_PNL` and `COMMISSION` match local fill components on symbol, native trade ID, asset and exact amount. Either arrival order changes cash once. An unmatched debit reserves funds and an unmatched credit remains unavailable.
* `ACCOUNT_UPDATE` retains sparse B/P rows, E/T times, balance changes (`bc`) and original position values. A lower reported wallet bounds available capital. `bc` alone cannot create a second cash flow. Duplicate observations have no additional debit. Unexplained observations keep the account frozen.

Tag 12 stores `IngressAccountInput`. Tag 13 stores `IncomeBaseline`. `IncomeReader` uses the existing TLS request, signed time and shared quota check at first acceptance. It requests at most 16 rows per page. `IncomeScan` permits 128 queries and splits saturated inclusive millisecond ranges. Saturation at a single millisecond means the scan is incomplete. `BaselineCoverage` preserves query evidence. Only Account mode creates account effects. The raw HTTP result, normalized rows and effects share one input batch. [Reader and scan](../../crates/tickwire-binance-account/src/reconciliation/income.rs)

A configured `IncomeBaselineAuthority` pins a dedicated risk administrator and Ed25519 key. A proposal is at most 54 KiB with up to 128 income rows and 128 references, including real account, open-order and conditional-order snapshots and their input digests. For initial import the three snapshots match the fixed baseline and span no account or order change. `RestOwner::import_income_baseline` binds the signature to the request, complete baseline, streams, writer, ledger position, clock and validity. The manifest references the prefix before approval. The account record's `through_sequence` also covers the synced approval. Retry returns the original result only for the same content. Recovery checks the original writer and acceptance time. Matching snapshots, short pages or Binance asset update times cannot grant baseline coverage. [Import authority](../../crates/tickwire-binance-account/src/reconciliation/income_import.rs)

An offline audit reports an input-only import in `input_facts_not_in_account` until the exact baseline has reached the account WAL. Matching wallet totals or cutoff times are insufficient. Reopen tests saw one unmatched effect after input sync, then zero after the matching account sync. The recovered account stayed frozen.

## Reconciliation and explicit association

The optional App `reconciliation.income_baseline_import` schedules actual queries, three snapshots and bounded polling for signed approval. It avoids duplicate queries while waiting, then requests fresh snapshots tied to the new ledger. The optional `income_account_scan` uses an explicit lookback and shares existing connection, signature and quota handling.

Tag 14, `AccountInputsReconciled`, changes all eligible post-baseline funding records from Booked to Reconciled and advances the ledger once. Its evidence binds scope, account epoch, ledger, baseline, history, input cut and verified response/snapshot digests. The reducer preserves cash, credit holds, capital limits and open-order liability. Exact retries cannot book again. Missing objects, unknown observations, Pending, conflicts, unknown pre-cutoff keys or mismatched snapshots reject. Reactivation requires a fresh set of all three snapshots at the new ledger and the usual gate checks. The scan never enlarges the original covered set.

Tag 15, `IncomeObservationsAssociated`, handles explicit one-use associations for all currently unexplained funding observations of one Cross wallet. Replay derives the opening wallet from the actual canonical baseline and ordered account prefix. Between anchor and cut it permits only the selected fees and observations, exact duplicates, the existing tag 14 and verified reactivations. Even an unrelated change that nets to zero invalidates the selection. All rows must satisfy per-asset `bc`, wallet and cross-wallet checks, the original private connection, empty position changes, complete Account-mode query coverage and fresh snapshots at the current ledger/input cut.

Approval binds the deployment root, signed leaf certificate, active policy digest/revision and leaf-signed manifest. App syncs the policy and approval with the acceptance time and physical writer in the input WAL, then syncs tag 15 after candidate validation. Generic `record_fact` rejects this tag. The record clears only the approved observations, consumes the association once and increments the ledger once. Cash, caps, holds, reservations, ownership, orders and baseline stay intact. An exact replay after policy expiry or leaf rotation uses the original authority. A new owner may complete an input-only record only from the same account prefix. Recovery verifies the six approval dependencies against the input and account histories.

The optional association scheduler reads regular files with bounded length, using `O_NOFOLLOW | O_NONBLOCK` on Unix. Missing files keep it waiting. After tag 15 it collects new snapshots, applies tag 14 if any rows remain Booked, then obtains fresh snapshots before reactivation. `ExternalTrade` can be encoded but its reducer rejects it. Unknown income types stay frozen. Liquidation, ADL, delivery, external adjustments, per-strategy funding attribution, root rotation and an approval issuance service remain outside this implementation.
