# Preparing a configuration plan against committed state

`ReplicaControl::begin_plan` accepts an authenticated principal, full candidate or scoped patch, If-Match and options. The caller cannot supply the current configuration, revision, Raft position or trust source. A successful internal `PlanDraft` pins those facts and contains the full candidate, projections and computed actions. It assigns no live revision, operation status or runtime permission. [Planning API](../../crates/tickwire-config-service/src/planning.rs)

**Read the baseline.** The replica starts a real ReadIndex round and waits for its business reducer through that barrier. A full candidate requires the current cluster Editor role. A patch must pass scope checks on all its selectors. The owner takes its `CommittedHead` and invokes `PreparedCandidate::prepare`. A `PendingPlan` belongs to the issuing replica and is single-use. Cancellation and terminal errors release its read ticket or completed result. A cancelled job still running retains its worker capacity.

**Compute against one source.** `Owner::start_plan` shares a bounded pool of 1–8 jobs with content validation. The worker uses the same immutable source bundle, artifact catalog, trust configuration and limits for `ContentValidated`, both sides of `ProjectionDelivery` and `ComputedPlan`. The digests must agree with the actual base and candidate. Workers and owner sample service time at acceptance, start, completion and result consumption. A clock regression, expiry, wrong owner or even a byte-identical replacement provider invalidates the outstanding result. Unconsumed results retain a capacity slot.

**Confirm the result.** A second ReadIndex round rechecks the full committed head, authenticated certificate, current roles, original scope, If-Match, source context and deadline. A changed baseline requires fresh preparation. The draft preserves the original principal and leaf digest. Its complete internal target set is not a scope-filtered HTTP response. It lists outstanding checks for `retained_registry_head`, `runtime_targets`, `runtime_apply_driver` and `durable_plan_admission`. Content archival is a separate operation and still leaves desired unchanged. [Plan archive](config-plan-archive.md)

Risk-budget tightening can be classified Hot by the pure planner. In this design the qualification reports bind the full business baseline. Renewing those references during an actual change currently introduces a cluster-scoped Quiesce action. A Hot field alone therefore cannot establish a deployable Hot operation.

Tests with three file-backed voters and real mTLS stopped the majority at each read, exercised stale ETag, absent Editor rights, revocation, provider replacement, cancellation and cross-owner reuse, then checked the unchanged desired head after success.
