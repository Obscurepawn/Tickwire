# Archived Design Note: October 2026 Clustered Binance Architecture

This placeholder marks the historical October 20, 2026 design document covering Tickwire's earlier multi-node clustered deployment for Binance Spot and USDⓈ-M Futures. Multi-node replication and cluster failover were subsequently removed in favor of the deterministic single-host architecture documented in [Single-Host Trading](implementation/single-host-trading.md).

For historical topology diagrams, quorum rules, and acceptance records from the clustered revision, consult the [archived October 20 design specification](https://github.com/Obscurepawn/Tickwire/blob/dc50649ebdcada26f7740181c16139cd3b32064a/specs/20261020-binance-ultra-low-latency.md).
