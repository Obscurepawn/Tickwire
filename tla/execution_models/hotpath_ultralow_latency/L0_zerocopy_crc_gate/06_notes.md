# Review and Phase-2 source-audit notes — `L0_ZeroCopyAndCrcGate`

1. **Lifetime-pinned zero-copy `&MarketRecord` safety**: `RecordBorrow<'a, B>` (`crates/tickwire-shm/src/mapped.rs:825-952`) withholds `Subscriber::release(lease)` until `Drop`, preventing the consumer's `RELEASED` watermark from advancing past `b.seq`. Strategy consumers in `crates/tickwire-market-source/src/shm.rs:283-296` and `crates/tickwire-strategy-sdk/src/worker.rs:701-735` explicitly drop the `RecordBorrow` guard before invoking strategy callbacks or `apply(seq)`. Because `Publisher` (`crates/tickwire-shm/src/mapped.rs:466-515`) claims slot `(next_seq - 1) % RingCapacity` only when `next_seq - cached_min_rel <= RingCapacity`, no producer write (`prod_state = "Writing"`) can ever overlap a slot currently borrowed as `&MarketRecord` (`ZeroNoAliasWhileBorrowed`).
2. **Arbitrary-bit-validity and ABI layout invariants**: `crates/tickwire-ipc/tools/generate.py:16-24`, `crates/tickwire-ipc/tools/generate.py:231-245`, and `crates/tickwire-ipc/src/wire.rs:4103-4215` enforce `#[repr(C)]`, `#[cfg(not(target_endian = "little"))] compile_error!(...)`, `size_of == 512`, `align_of == 8`, exhaustive `offset_of!` assertions, and explicit `_pad_*` integer/byte arrays with zero `bool` or `enum` fields across `RecordHeader` (`128`B), `DepthLevel` (`16`B), `MarketBody` (`384`B), `MarketRecord` (`512`B), and `MarketExactRecord` (`512`B). `crates/tickwire-ipc/src/zerocopy.rs:46-286` further verifies 8-byte pointer alignment (`align_offset == 0`), zeroed padding bytes, schema/message-type whitelists, and conditional `flags & 8` + `crc32c_masked(bytes, 112..120)` before forming `&MarketRecord` (`ZeroCopyLayoutAndHeaderSoundness`, `CrcGateModeConsistency`).
3. **Physical WAL recovery without hot-path `CRC32C`**: Even when `not(feature = "crc32")` writes `header_crc32c = 0` and `payload_crc32c = 0`, `crates/tickwire-journal/src/frame.rs:108-193` unconditionally computes and verifies `Sha256(b[..tail] || b[tail + 32..])` plus `repeated_total_len` and `END_MAGIC` (`b"TEND"`), while `crates/tickwire-journal/src/frame.rs:146-153` (`total % 8 == 0` and `total - 176 - payload < 8`) and `crates/tickwire-journal/src/journal.rs:292-304` (`BatchSeal` `payload_len == 0`) mathematically prevent a single-bit flip in `BatchSeal.total` from inflating `total > remaining.len()` to masquerade as an unsealed torn tail (`WalRecoverySoundWithoutCrc`).
