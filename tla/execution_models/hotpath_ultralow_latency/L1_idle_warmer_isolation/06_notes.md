# Review notes — `L1_IdleWarmerIsolation`

- **Stateless `AccountSession` `dry_run` isolation**: `AccountSession` exits early under `dry_run` before mutating balances, open-order holds, `StpBook`, sequence floors, or SHM `ReportRecord` rings (`crates/tickwire-runtime/src/session.rs:663-690`), while `BinanceHotPathWarmer` and `UtaHotPathWarmer` format and sign into stack scratch buffers (`crates/tickwire-binance-execution/src/warmup.rs:282-468`, `crates/tickwire-spot-execution/src/warmup.rs:296-425`).
- **Why loopback `rustls` pair is mandatory**: Encrypting into `rustls::ClientConnection` advances the AEAD write record counter; discarding that ciphertext desynchronizes the next live venue frame (`bad_record_mac`), which `TlsLoopbackWarmer` eliminates by maintaining an isolated offline `ClientConnection` + `ServerConnection` loopback pair (`crates/tickwire-transport/src/warmup.rs:219-422`).
- **64-bit clock & unbounded sequence lifting**: Full `0 .. 2^64 - 1` `WarmupTracker` arithmetic, cold-startup `CLOCK_MONOTONIC` seeding, and non-zero idle poll waits are proved symbolically in [../L3_sym_ring_warmer_inductive/05_output.md](../L3_sym_ring_warmer_inductive/05_output.md) and [../L5_sym_warmer_crc_fix_validation/05_output.md](../L5_sym_warmer_crc_fix_validation/05_output.md).
