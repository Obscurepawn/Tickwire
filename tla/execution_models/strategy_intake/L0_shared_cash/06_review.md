# Independent review of cross-member cancellation

At source revision `9b2ed63`, member B can stop the cash account owner by submitting a cancel bearing B’s own authenticated strategy identity and the exact original identifiers of A’s admitted order. The foreign cancel never reaches the venue. An otherwise valid request from A remains in its queue when the process exits. This is an availability violation of the project’s worker-isolation acceptance case. It requires access to a registered queue and knowledge of the target identifiers. It does not establish that an unrelated external process can write that queue or discover the identifiers. [Worker isolation acceptance](../../../../specs/implementation/single-host-trading.md#acceptance) · [Strategy scheduling guide](../../../../crates/tickwire-app/AGENTS.md)

* `MultiStrategy::next` authenticates the submitting member and instance at `crates/tickwire-app/src/strategy.rs:628-671`. Using B’s real identity satisfies that guard. The runner resolves the target against the order index and the immutable original command in `crates/tickwire-spot-runner/src/lib.rs:202-249`. A matching known original satisfies the origin checks. Supplying a venue order ID is optional.
* `Account::check_target` rejects the differing strategy or manager at `crates/tickwire-account/src/admission.rs:369-397`. The candidate account is discarded. Its error rule at lines 16–30 freezes on conflict or arithmetic overflow; a target identity refusal is an ordinary error there. `crates/tickwire-account-gateway/src/lib.rs:413-435` returns before an admission record or queued transport bytes. There is no unknown send or corruption in this trace.
* `crates/tickwire-spot-runner/src/lib.rs:126-147,266-280` reports the result to the member, then propagates this error. `crates/tickwire-spot-runner/src/live.rs:342` propagates it out of the owner loop and `crates/tickwire-spot-runner/src/main.rs:279-286` exits with failure. A later external process restart cannot preserve uninterrupted service to A.
* The spot routing specification explicitly promises continued operation after three unfrozen resource refusals. It separately specifies a sender stop for a *conflicting origin*. This witness has a verified matching original and fails solely on management authority. The source authorizes B’s process, not management of A’s order. Localizing that typed refusal to B would preserve order ownership and the documented worker isolation guarantee. Blindly swallowing all identity errors would also swallow unrelated integrity failures. [Spot routing contract](../../../../specs/implementation/spot-strategy-routing.md#submit-and-observe)

**Disposition.** Confirmed cross-member denial of service in the file-configured Bybit/OKX live path. The account rejects unauthorized management correctly. This review does not generalize to Binance or to conflicts that actually freeze the account.
