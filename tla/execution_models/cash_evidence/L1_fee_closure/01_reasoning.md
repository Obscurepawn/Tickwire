# Write and replay boundaries

A terminal report retains the greatest observed cumulative quantity. The REST scan can carry a smaller value even when it agrees with the individually booked executions and with the wallet. The owner validates the proposed closure against the retained terminal quantity and booked quantity before writing the record. One exclusive owner holds the account through the write and applies the validated transition after sync. A crash between sync and publication rebuilds the same decision from the journal. The historical model shows the original ordering. [Owner](../../../../crates/tickwire-account-gateway/src/lib.rs#L1026) · [Fee reducer](../../../../crates/tickwire-account/src/admission.rs#L484)

The new-input profiles admit one or two distinct trades and any terminal quantity from zero to that limit. Each trade costs two quote units and one fee unit. The owner keeps at most one outstanding scan. The model abstracts file sync to a durable prefix and keeps order identity, scale, spot instrument and sequence capacity fixed. A crash changes volatile state only. The malformed historical fixture has one terminal report, no observed contradiction and no booked executions. The compatible fixture adds the lower second terminal report that set the disagreement flag. Applying either fixture checks replay against the exact order of its stored rows.
