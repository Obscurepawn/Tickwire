# Owners and observable steps

* The publisher makes a complete quote available; the two producers and account consume it in independently scheduled steps. One valid quote, one market generation, one boot clock, a configured strategy grant and an authenticated dedicated account are inputs. Positive market and private-stream age checks hold except for the modeled change in private status. [Quote publication and consumption](../../quote_delivery/L0_pair/05_output.md)
* Each member supplies one known client ID, price and quantity. One full reservation pays for one buy and its fee. The state tracks free cash independently from outstanding reservations, booked trades and total debits. A REST acknowledgement carries no money. A venue may accept after the account crashes. It can fill before the client receives an acknowledgement. A later private delivery of a REST-booked trade preserves the debit count. Multi-asset arithmetic, partial fills and ambiguous external cash movements require the economic model or native tests.
* Admission represents the successful closure and WAL append. The old account-gateway model splits the physical write. The current account cannot poll other account-owned work between sampling the gate and attempting TLS. A private reader can capture a fill or observe EOF there. The authorization witness records what the owner sampled. The transport rereads the command deadline before acceptance. The wire counter counts the first locally accepted byte, not delivery to the venue.
* BeginScan records the cut. Activate projects the successful result of two matching scans plus exact account comparison. A change in the durable facts or in the exchange observations invalidates this modeled scan. This models stable data and a successful coherent observation. Real REST reads are paginated and have per-query cutoffs; the composition makes no cross-endpoint linearizability assumption about the exchange in general. Failed recognition of an admitted order can leave the account waiting for venue evidence.
* Restart projects a crash, a successful WAL replay, a new approved writer and successful re-JOIN/re-authentication. Exchange effects and reservations survive. The new process has no pending transport attempt or grant. A rejected historical WAL record lies outside this successful-replay branch. [Terminal evidence and restart](../../cash_evidence/L0_terminal_fees/05_output.md)
* Safety configurations admit arbitrarily slow participants, one observed private gap, a deadline expiry and, for the restart configuration, one crash. Progress uses weak fairness for the nominated producer, owner and venue steps in a fault-free run with adequate funds. It assumes a marketable order that the venue accepts and eventually fills. Exchange matching, TLS scheduling and the time between polls have no production liveness guarantee here.
