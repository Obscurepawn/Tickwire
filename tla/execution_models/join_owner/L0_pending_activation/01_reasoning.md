# L0 state and abstractions

Each registration entry tracks phase, fixed baseline `S`, birth membership version `V`, completion/delivery flags, cursor registration, dual `READ_READY` acknowledgements, activation watermark, and final `Quiesce` watermarks (`closedRel`, `closedApp`). Global membership increments only on join and detach, and `JOIN_READY` validates each entry's original birth `V` regardless of concurrent joins by other subscribers.

The publisher reclamation gate is the minimum across all non-detached entries: `Pending` / `Expired` entries hold at baseline `S`, while `Active` entries expose their contiguous `released` prefix. Every physical slot retains its written sequence number so invariants verify live borrows and unread records directly against slot contents (`Capacity = 64`, `Subscriber<1>` window with independent `released` / `applied` ordering).

`prepare` splits into entry registration (`busy` lock) and snapshot/offer completion while existing subscribers continue advancing cursors concurrently; delivery failure or deadline expiration transitions unactivated entries without detaching their baseline `S` gate. Duplicate `JOIN_READY` on an `Active` entry idempotently returns the first activation watermark without re-checking catch-up against newer publications, and duplicate `Quiesce` ACKs after detachment compare against stored `(closedRel, closedApp)` even if the reader later advances `applied`.
