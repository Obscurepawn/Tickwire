# Cash and exposure in the live account

At source revision 9b2ed63 the file-configured spot runner submits to a real account gateway. Candidate admission calls Account::evaluate. Its asset view invokes tickwire_risk::spot::available and intersects the result with account-effective spendability. The same evaluation checks maximum position, gross quantity and per-strategy limits. The existing account economic model uses fixed integral buy fills. This level examines independently signed remaining orders, partial executions, negative virtual strategy ownership, a baseline lock and the resulting account-wide and per-member exposure envelope.
