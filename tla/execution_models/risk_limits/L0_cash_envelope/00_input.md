# Cash and exposure in the live account

Outbound spot order submission routes through the account gateway and per-symbol risk engine before wire dispatch. Candidate admission calls `Account::evaluate` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/calculation.rs:474-501`) alongside per-symbol `RmsEngine::check_order` (`crates/tickwire-risk/src/rms.rs:1192-1255`). Its asset view (`crates/tickwire-account/src/calculation.rs:265-304`) invokes `tickwire_risk::spot::available` (`crates/tickwire-risk/src/spot.rs:134-182`) and intersects the result with account-effective spendability (`crates/tickwire-account/src/account_inputs.rs:545-566`). The same evaluation checks maximum position, gross quantity, and per-strategy limits (`crates/tickwire-account/src/calculation.rs:502-582`). This level examines independently signed remaining orders, partial executions, negative virtual strategy ownership, a baseline lock, and the resulting account-wide and per-member exposure envelope.
