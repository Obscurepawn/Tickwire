# Multi-connection router and quota follow-up

All six Stage 3D cross-layer findings documented in [`L0_pool_router/04_counterexamples.md`](L0_pool_router/04_counterexamples.md) and [`L1_multi_ip_quota/04_counterexamples.md`](L1_multi_ip_quota/04_counterexamples.md) have been resolved and covered by regression tests across `tickwire-transport`, `tickwire-account-gateway`, `tickwire-quota`, `tickwire-binance-account`, `tickwire-spot-execution`, and `tickwire-spot-runner`:

1. **Resolved (Bug 1)**: `OrderConnectionPool::poll_dials_filtered` and `AccountGateway::poll_pool_dials_filtered` gate ready `DialBaton` installation on `pending_send.is_none()`, `retained_receive.is_empty()`, and owner `slot.pending.is_none()`, preventing premature epoch increment or `retained_receive` clobber before pre-EOF replies and stale `SlotPending`/`SpotPending` entries are drained.
2. **Resolved (Bug 2)**: `RestOwner::poll_pooled_rest` and `RestSession::drive_pool` reap `Status::Aborted` pending entries immediately after `poll_slot` (before reading socket bytes) and omit duplicate `release_slot_inflight` calls on `Status::Aborted` (already released inside `AccountGateway::apply_slot_report(SendState::NoBytes)`).
3. **Resolved (Bug 3)**: `WsOwner` latches `Runtime.draining_eof` to drain complete pre-EOF frames buffered under byte/message poll caps before isolating remaining `Flushed` entries, and `AccountGateway::check_control_report` drops isolated WS `Close` flushes via `drop_broken_transport_isolated` instead of halting under `per_order_doubt_isolation`.
4. **Resolved (Bug 4)**: `RestOwner::submit`, `RestOwner::submit_pooled_rest`, and `WsOwner::submit_websocket` precheck `can_afford_quota_on_ip` before `Account::admit` and commit mutating `allow_quota_*` debits only after gateway submission succeeds (`status != Status::Aborted`), preventing local risk rejections from burning exchange `ORDER` and `IP` windows.
5. **Resolved (Bug 5)**: `RateLimiter::can_afford` tracks cumulative per-window `prior_units` across duplicate `RateLimitId` spends, widens `RollingEntry.units` and `WindowUndo` to `u64` with overflow-safe `checked_add` and `u128` retry deficit arithmetic, and aligns `VenueRateLimiter` unseen-symbol `OkxSpot` and `BinanceUsdm` `weight == 0` probes with `allow_*`.
6. **Resolved (Bug 6)**: `RestSession` and `AccountRunner` reap direct gateway `Status::Aborted` slots, retire `Origins` on post-`register_command` submit errors, and skip duplicate `origins.register` on byte-identical retries.
