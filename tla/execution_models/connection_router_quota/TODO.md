# Multi-connection router and quota follow-up

All six cross-layer findings documented in [`L0_pool_router/04_counterexamples.md`](L0_pool_router/04_counterexamples.md) and [`L1_multi_ip_quota/04_counterexamples.md`](L1_multi_ip_quota/04_counterexamples.md) are resolved and covered by regression tests across `tickwire-transport`, `tickwire-account-gateway`, `tickwire-quota`, `tickwire-binance-quota`, `tickwire-binance-account`, and `tickwire-spot-execution`:

1. **Resolved (Bug 1)**: `OrderConnectionPool::poll_dials_filtered` and `AccountGateway::poll_pool_dials_filtered` gate ready `DialBaton` installation on `pending_send.is_none()`, `retained_receive.is_empty()`, and session slot drain completion, preventing premature epoch increment or `retained_receive` clobber before pre-EOF replies are drained.
2. **Resolved (Bug 2)**: Session slots reap `Status::Aborted` pending entries before reading socket bytes and omit duplicate `release_slot_inflight` calls on `Status::Aborted` (released once inside `AccountGateway::apply_slot_report(SendState::NoBytes)`).
3. **Resolved (Bug 3)**: Multiplexed WebSocket sessions finish draining complete pre-EOF frames buffered under poll caps before isolating remaining `Flushed` entries, and `AccountGateway::check_control_report` isolates broken WS `Close` flushes via `drop_broken_transport_isolated`.
4. **Resolved (Bug 4)**: Outbound sessions commit mutating quota debits only after local order admission and wire encoding succeed, preventing local rejections from burning exchange `ORDER` and `IP` windows.
5. **Resolved (Bug 5)**: `RateLimiter::can_afford` tracks cumulative per-window `prior_units` across duplicate `RateLimitId` spends, widens `RollingEntry.units` and `WindowUndo` to `u64` with overflow-safe `checked_add` and `u128` retry deficit arithmetic, and aligns `VenueRateLimiter` probes with `allow_*`.
6. **Resolved (Bug 6)**: Unified venue sessions reap aborted pending slots and release claimed route inflight on failed or rejected sends.
