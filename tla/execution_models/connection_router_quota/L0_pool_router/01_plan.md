# Modeling plan — `L0_PoolRouter`

## Scope and state owners
- **`OrderRouter`, `PublishedRoutes`, & `OrderConnectionPool`** (`crates/tickwire-transport/src/order_router.rs:130-232`, `378-515`, `688-745`, `941-1015`): owns per-slot `SendSlot` (`Exclusive` vs. `Shared`), `established`, `epoch` increments on `on_disconnect` and `on_connected`, `inflight` counters (`acquire_inflight` / epoch-fenced `release_inflight`), integer EWMA (`(ema_us * 7 + sample_us) / 8`), warmup round-robin over `last_conn_id`, and `9/8` sticky hysteresis (`last_score * 8 <= best_score * 9`).
- **`AccountGateway` pool** (`crates/tickwire-account-gateway/src/pool.rs:290-365`, `506-540`, `690-835`, `crates/tickwire-account-gateway/src/websocket.rs:209-225`): owns per-slot `pending_send`, `retained_receive` capture on isolated slot drops (`drop_broken_slot_isolated`), zero-byte `SendState::NoBytes` abort + inflight release vs. `MayHaveSent`/`Flushed` doubt isolation, and `poll_pool_dials_filtered` guards (`pending_send.is_none() && retained_receive.is_empty()`).
- **Unified venue sessions (`UnifiedBinanceSession` & `UnifiedUtaSession`)** (`crates/tickwire-binance-account/src/session.rs:857-955`, `crates/tickwire-spot-execution/src/session.rs:417-470`, `937-980`, `1235-1295`): own per-slot in-flight correlation, pre-read `Status::Aborted` reaping without duplicate `release_slot_inflight`, multiplexed out-of-order `WsRequestId` / `reply_id` correlation, pre-EOF frame draining, and post-EOF `InDoubt` + `OrsQueryDesk` synchronization.

Exercises out-of-order `WsRequestId` completions, mixed `Flushed` + `Queued(0-byte)` WS disconnect isolation, and partial pre-EOF draining before `InDoubt` finalization.
