# Modeling plan — `L0_PoolRouter`

## Scope and state owners
- **`OrderRouter` & `OrderConnectionPool`** (`crates/tickwire-transport/src/order_router.rs:18-444`, `630-746`): owns per-slot `SendSlot` (`Exclusive` vs. `Shared`), `established`, `epoch` increments on `on_disconnect` and `on_connected`, `inflight` counters (`acquire_inflight` / epoch-fenced `release_inflight`), integer EWMA (`(ema_us * 7 + sample_us) / 8`), warmup round-robin over `last_conn_id`, and `9/8` sticky hysteresis (`last_score * 8 <= best_score * 9`).
- **`AccountGateway` pool** (`crates/tickwire-account-gateway/src/pool.rs:288-801`, `crates/tickwire-account-gateway/src/websocket.rs:200-226`): owns per-slot `pending_send`, `retained_receive` capture on isolated slot drops (`drop_broken_slot_isolated`), zero-byte `SendState::NoBytes` abort + inflight release vs. `MayHaveSent`/`Flushed` doubt isolation, and `poll_pool_dials_filtered` guards (`pending_send.is_none() && retained_receive.is_empty()`).
- **`RestOwner`, `RestSession`, `AccountRunner`, & `WsOwner`** (`crates/tickwire-binance-account/src/rest.rs:235-270`, `581-1092`, `crates/tickwire-binance-account/src/ws/owner.rs:259-360`, `455-735`, `crates/tickwire-spot-execution/src/session.rs:263-306`, `711-1269`, `crates/tickwire-spot-runner/src/lib.rs:136-159`, `264-465`): own post-flush `SlotPending`/`SpotPending`, pre-read `Status::Aborted` reaping without duplicate `release_slot_inflight`, multiplexed out-of-order `WsRequestId` correlation, `Runtime.draining_eof` pre-EOF frame draining, and post-EOF `InDoubt` + `QueryDesk` synchronization.
