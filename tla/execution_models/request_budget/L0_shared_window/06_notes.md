# Review notes

A grant whose acknowledgement is lost still counts against its original window. Wall-clock expiry alone leaves the retained units intact. A grant prepared for the successor window cannot charge the predecessor; an unspent predecessor grant cannot fund an attempt in the successor. The [Binance adapter](../../../../crates/tickwire-binance-quota/src/lib.rs#L343) supplies stable typed request identities and costs. The dedicated Bybit and OKX spot reader does not pass through this Binance adapter.
