# Per-order `QueryDesk` follow-up

All ten Stage 2D cross-layer findings documented in [`L0_per_order_isolation/04_counterexamples.md`](L0_per_order_isolation/04_counterexamples.md) and [`L1_query_convergence/04_counterexamples.md`](L1_query_convergence/04_counterexamples.md) have been resolved and covered by regression tests across `tickwire-account`, `tickwire-binance-account`, and `tickwire-spot-runner`:

1. **Resolved (Bugs A1 & A2)**: `Account::reopen_order` restores pre-`Reactivated` Futures `shadow_holds` on contradicted `uncreated`/`aborted` orders, and `Account::evict_expired` folds unrefreshed `shadow_holds` into `uncovered_debits`.
2. **Resolved (Bug B)**: Spot `PerOrderQueryDriver::handle_reply` routes zero-byte `RestStatus::Expired | RestStatus::NotBuilt` to `QueryDesk::on_not_sent`.
3. **Resolved (Bugs C, D, H)**: `QueryDesk` preserves and promotes `trades_after` and `cancel_after` across `on_not_sent`, `on_transport_failure`, pre-grace `on_not_found`, `on_status_snapshot`, `on_trades_applied`, and `poll_due`, counts confirmed `on_not_found` toward `max_query_count`, enqueues `AskKind::Trades` on Binance WS `FillCatchup`, and tolerates out-of-order stale open REST snapshots.
4. **Resolved (Bugs E, F, G, I)**: Spot `dispatch_due` and `dispatch_bybit_history` enforce `in_flight` + `is_ticket_in_flight` guards against mid-flight WS `remove_order`; both drivers clear `CancelAmbiguous` upon `CancelRejectedStillOpen { remaining_pending_cancels: 0 }`; non-terminal WS partial fills preserve `CancelAmbiguous`; and Spot `handle_order_not_found` isolates `DoubtReason::FillCatchup` on confirmed `FetchTradesBeforeClose`.
