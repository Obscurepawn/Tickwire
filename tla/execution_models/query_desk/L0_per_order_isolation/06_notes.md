# Review notes

1. **Transient `in_doubt` vs. durable `RecoveryRecord` boundary**: `Order.in_doubt` and `Order.retired_mono_ns` are intentionally excluded from `PartialEq for Order` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/lib.rs:84-119`) and `AccountJournal` records. Cold or crash recovery (`Account::recover`) keeps unclosed `Admit` reservations locked by construction until authenticated venue reconciliation (`Order`, `Fill`, `Rejected`, `FeesReconciled`, or `Reactivated`) resolves them.
2. **Pre-`Reactivated` Futures `shadow_holds` invariant**: Both `Account::reopen_order` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/lib.rs:957-967`) and `Account::evict_expired` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/lib.rs:924-928`) preserve the invariant that every admitted, non-uncreated, non-aborted Futures order since `last_reactivation_sequence` contributes its initial margin hold to either `shadow_holds` or `uncovered_debits`.
3. **Duplicate `VenueEventBody::Rejected` idempotency**: Once an order is closed as `uncreated = true` (via outbound synchronous reject or post-grace `CloseUncreated`), subsequent duplicate `Rejected` facts return `Applied::Duplicate` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/events.rs:644-646`) without setting `frozen` or mutating `retired_mono_ns`.
