# State ownership, solvency conservation, and replay parity

Under Stage 2 (`per_order_doubt_isolation = true`), `AccountGateway` separates link liveness from whole-account halt when a transport error or single-order timeout affects an outbound command after `accepted_bytes > 0`. While `accepted_bytes == 0` (`Queued`), `AbortUnsentBeforeFirstByte` commits a durable `RecoveryRecord::Aborted` and releases reservations immediately. Once the first plaintext byte enters TLS (`MayHaveSent`), a socket drop invokes `drop_broken_transport_isolated`: only that `MayHaveSent` entry transitions to `Status::Unknown` with `DoubtReason::PlaceAmbiguous` in `Account`, while `Flushed` sibling orders remain untouched, `halted` stays `false`, and `install_transport_connection` adopts the next pre-warmed `Dialer` link without requiring full-account `Reactivated`.

Economic safety hinges on three coupled mechanisms inside `Account`:

1. **Grace-gated uncreated closure (`NoPrematureUncreatedBeforeGrace` & `ReservationLockedWhileInDoubt`)**: While an unconfirmed order is in `PlaceAmbiguous`, its full Spot principal (`Order::remaining`), Futures `shadow_holds`, and derivative `reduce` exposure remain locked, and `Order::is_retirable` checks `self.in_doubt.is_none()` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/lib.rs:173`) so `evict_expired` cannot reclaim the order. `QueryNotFoundBeforeGrace` leaves all holds intact so an order queued inside the venue matching engine (`VenueIndexLate`: `"PendingIndex" -> "Open"`) never races a sibling into double-spending `WalletCap`. Only an explicit venue business reject (`DeliverDeterministicReject`) or `NotFound` strictly at/after `first_doubt_mono_ns + not_found_grace_ns` (`QueryNotFoundAfterGrace`) records `VenueEventBody::Rejected` (`uncreated = true, fees_closed = true`).
2. **Contradicted-uncreated reopening (`ContradictedUncreatedFreezes`)**: If an adversarial or out-of-envelope venue delay delivers a late acknowledgement or fill after `uncreated = true`, `Account::reopen_order` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/lib.rs:957-967`) clears `uncreated`, `aborted`, `fees_closed`, and `retired_mono_ns`, latches `observation_unreconciled = true` and `frozen = true`, restores `reduce`, and—when no `Reactivated` wallet snapshot has intervened since `admitted_sequence`—re-inserts the order's initial Futures `shadow_holds` so `venue_shadow` reflects the live liability immediately.
3. **Shadow-preserving eviction and WAL replay parity (`ReplayEquivalence`)**: Transient `in_doubt` transitions mutate only in-memory fields excluded from `PartialEq for Order` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/lib.rs:84-119`), whereas every economic transition (`SubmitOrder`, `AbortUnsentBeforeFirstByte`, `DeliverDeterministicReject`, `QueryNotFoundAfterGrace`, `DeliverVenueFlatCancel`, `DeliverVenueFill`, `ReconcileOrderFees`, `AdversarialLateFactAfterUncreated`) appends a durable `RecoveryRecord`. When `EvictExpired` reclaims a retired order prior to `Reactivated`, folding its remaining `shadow_holds` entry into `uncovered_debits` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/lib.rs:924-928`) keeps `TotalShadowDeduction` identical to an un-evicted replay reducer.
