# Review notes

1. **Five orthogonal non-retirable guards**: `Order::is_retirable` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/lib.rs:158-167`) and `Account::is_order_retirable` (`:594-603`) block window expiry whenever an order is live resting (`!aborted && !terminal`), carries `unknown` venue status, awaits fee closure (`!fees_closed`), has non-zero `remaining()` (`terminal_cumulative != Some(filled)`, such as a terminal `OrderStatus` arriving ahead of its trade execution), or has an unresolved amendment in flight.
2. **Fact-coupled order retention**: `Account::intent_retired_mono_ns` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/lib.rs:559-582`) and `facts_for_order_expired` (`:740-753`) take the maximum timestamp across `o.retired_mono_ns` and every associated `FactEntry.recorded_mono_ns`, and `AccountGateway::refresh_completed_entries` (`crates/tickwire-account-gateway/src/lib.rs:582-586`) incorporates `intent_retired_mono_ns` into `completed_mono_ns`. Consequently, trade-ID deduplication remains intact for at least `Window` after the final fill and never desynchronizes gateway and reducer eviction.
3. **Management pool isolation**: Keeping `MAX_MANAGEMENT_ADMISSIONS` (`4,096`) disjoint from `MAX_ORDERS` (`1,024`) guarantees that `Cancel` and `Query` traffic can still be admitted and flushed when open orders saturate every order-creating slot; see [counterexamples and candidate notes](04_counterexamples.md) for the three candidate edge cases regarding heterogeneous management TTLs, confirmed `AmendKeepPriority` accumulation, and pre-compaction `Account` cloning.
