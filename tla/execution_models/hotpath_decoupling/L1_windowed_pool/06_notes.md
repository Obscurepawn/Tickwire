# Review notes

1. **Orthogonal non-retirable guards**: `Order::is_retirable` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/lib.rs:282-291`) and `Account::is_order_retirable` (`:844-860`) block window expiry whenever an order is live resting (`!aborted && !terminal`), carries `unknown` venue status, is isolated `in_doubt` after a partial/ambiguous socket disconnect (`crates/tickwire-account-gateway/src/lib.rs:1343-1475`), has non-zero `remaining()` (`terminal_cumulative != Some(filled)`), or has an unresolved amendment/modification in flight.
2. **Two-tier `FillRecordPool` + `Account.facts` deduplication and `amendment = true` fee patching**: `FillRecordPool::record_or_amend` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/fill_record.rs:171-277`) suppresses duplicate `trade_id` deliveries in `O(1)` inside `[recorded_mono_ns, recorded_mono_ns + retention_ns)`, upgrades `TRADE_LITE` (`fee == None`) or post-trade fee/rebate revisions into `FillRecordOutcome::Amended` (`amendment = true`) without re-applying fill quantity, and reuses only expired associative ways (`age_ns >= retention_ns`). Concurrently, `Account::apply_event_candidate` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/events.rs:246-359`) reverses prior fee deltas before applying revised fees, refreshes `FactEntry.recorded_mono_ns`, and couples `intent_retired_mono_ns` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/lib.rs:792-819`) + `facts_for_order_expired` (`:1011-1024`) so order-level fact deduplication remains resident until both the order and its latest fee amendment expire.
3. **Management pool isolation**: Keeping `MAX_MANAGEMENT_ADMISSIONS` (`4,096`) disjoint from `MAX_ORDERS` (`1,024`) guarantees that `Cancel` and `Query` traffic can still be admitted and flushed when open orders saturate every order-creating slot.
