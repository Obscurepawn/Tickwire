# Active-protected windowed eviction and journal prefix continuity

Across both exhaustive breadth-first searches, no non-retirable order—whether live resting, marked `unknown`, awaiting `fees_closed`, holding non-zero `remaining()` (`terminal_cumulative != Some(filled)`), or guarded by an in-flight amendment—was ever evicted from `Account` or `AccountGateway` as `now` advanced across retention windows (`ActiveNeverEvicted`). Inside `[retired_at, retired_at + Window)` and `[recorded_at, recorded_at + Window)`, byte-identical `IntentKey` retries and duplicate `trade_id` fills hit resident deduplication entries with zero double-admissions or double-booked fills (`WindowDedupPreserved`). Even when live resting orders occupied every `MaxOrders` slot, `Cancel`/`Query` admissions remained available via independent `MaxMgmt` headroom and eager retired-management reclamation (`ManagementNeverStarved`). Prefix compaction (`maybe_compact_journal` and `compact_before`) preserved unbroken sequence numbers (`evicted_prefix + Len(records) == total_appends`), rolling `HistoryFrontier` equivalence, and retained `AdmissionOrigin` slots (`AdmissionSlot::Retained`) for all active and still-resident intents (`FrontierContinuity`).
