# Modeling plan — `L0_StpModify`

## Scope and state owners
- **`StpBook` & `SymbolSlot`** (`crates/tickwire-account/src/stp_book.rs:43-105`, `174-395`, `443-450`): owns per-side order vectors (`buy_orders`, `sell_orders`), dual-price `aggressive_ticks` (`max(resting, pending)` for `Buy`, `min(resting, pending)` for `Sell`), `Vec::swap_remove` slot compaction + `recompute_best_bid`/`recompute_best_ask`, and O(1) `self_cross` / `self_cross_excluding` checks.
- **`Account` `Modify` & hold engine** (`crates/tickwire-account/src/modifications.rs:11-385`, `430-894`, `crates/tickwire-account/src/calculation.rs:115-175`, `crates/tickwire-account/src/events.rs:499-625`): owns single-in-flight `PendingModify`, `ModificationRecord`, worst-case Spot quote/base holds and Futures `shadow_holds`, `client_id` tentative indexing and alias promotion, venue-clamped `observe_modified`, `abort_modification` / `reject_modification` rollback, and `supersede_pending_modify_on_terminal` when a fill or cancel closes an order mid-modify.
- **Execution gate, `Origins`, & `PerOrderQueryDriver`** (`crates/tickwire-spot-execution/src/session.rs:730-780`, `crates/tickwire-spot-runner/src/lib.rs:285-335`, `crates/tickwire-binance-account/src/query_desk_driver.rs:420-690`, `crates/tickwire-binance-account/src/modify.rs:604-815`): own pre-wire local refusal non-burn (`Status::Aborted` skipping `allow_command` and pre-registration `Account::admit` precheck) and `AmendAmbiguous` per-order query resolution (including Spot two-phase `origClientOrderId = new_client_id` -> immediate `-2013` fallback to `orderId`).
