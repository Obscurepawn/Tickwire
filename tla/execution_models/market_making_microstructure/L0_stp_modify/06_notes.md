# Review notes

1. **Dual-price `aggressive_ticks` closes simultaneous-modify STP races**: `StpBook::aggressive_ticks` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/stp_book.rs:443-450`) stores `max(resting, pending)` on `Buy` and `min(resting, pending)` on `Sell` inside `SymbolSlot` (`best_bid_ticks` / `best_ask_ticks`). While a `Buy` modify `1 -> 2` is in flight (`best_bid = 2`), a concurrent opposite-side `Sell` `3 -> 2` is immediately rejected by `self_cross_excluding` (`2 <= best_bid`), even if the venue later rejects or clamps either modify.
2. **Worst-case hold across Spot and Futures**: Spot `order_resources` reserves `max(resting_hold, pending_hold)` directly on the order, whereas Futures `admit_modify_candidate` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/modifications.rs:286-355`) also records `delta = max(0, after_capital - before_capital)` in `shadow_holds[mod_intent]` against `venue_shadow(wallet)`, releasing unused excess on venue-clamped `Modified` (`682-721`) and full `shadow_holds[mod_intent]` on `Aborted`, `ModifyRejected`, or `supersede_pending_modify_on_terminal`.
3. **Spot `cancelReplace` two-phase query resolution**: Because Binance Spot `cancelReplace` cancels `orig_order_id` and creates a new `orderId` under `new_client_id`, `PerOrderQueryDriver` (`crates/tickwire-binance-account/src/query_desk_driver.rs:420-690`) must probe `origClientOrderId = new_client_id` before falling back to `orderId` on `-2013`; querying `orig_order_id` first would observe `CANCELED` even when the replacement leg is live.
