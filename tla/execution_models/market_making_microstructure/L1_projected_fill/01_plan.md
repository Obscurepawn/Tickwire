# Modeling plan — `L1_ProjectedFill`

## Scope and state owners
- **`Account::on_cum_qty` & `FactKey` fill ledger** (`crates/tickwire-account/src/projected_fill.rs:60-280`, `crates/tickwire-account/src/events.rs:84-175`): owns settled `o.filled` and `facts` (`trade_id` deduplication), status watermarks (`exch_cum_qty`, `exch_quote_qty`, `exch_avg_qty`, `projected_cum_qty`), live-order and `cum_qty <= exch_cum_qty` guards, `CHECK_STP` opposite-side `self_cross` projection suppression, and `ProjectedFill` gap-quantity / gap-price derivation.
- **Strategy `FillWatermarkTracker`** (`crates/tickwire-strategy-sdk/src/microstructure.rs:622-655`, `770-1065`, `1205-1277`): owns per-order `confirmed_filled`, `projected_filled`, `exch_cum_qty`, `exch_quote_qty`, `exch_avg_qty`, bounded `seen_trades` `trade_id` deduplication, retirement gating, and incremental `effective_delta` emission across `on_projected_fill`, `on_cumulative_quantity`, and `on_fill_fixed`.

## Configurations and invariants
1. **`L1_ProjectedFill.cfg` (Multi-trade out-of-order status/fill interleavings + `LimitFallback`/`CumulativeQuote` denominator + `Modify` qty increase)**: `Trades = {1, 2, 3}`, `TradePrice = 10`, `OrderLimitPx = 10`, `InitialQty = 2`, `MaxModifiedQty = 3`, `CheckStp = FALSE`, `InitOppCross = FALSE`. Exercises settled-first fills, status-first projections, mixed `LimitFallback` + `CumulativeQuote` incremental price denominators, stale status frames, duplicate `trade_id` replays, `Modify` `2 -> 3` after partial fill, and post-terminal status suppression.
2. **`L1_ProjectedFill_SelfCross.cfg` (`CHECK_STP` opposite-side `self_cross` suppression + cancel unblocking)**: `Trades = {1, 2}`, `TradePrice = 10`, `OrderLimitPx = 10`, `InitialQty = 2`, `MaxModifiedQty = 2`, `CheckStp = TRUE`, `InitOppCross = TRUE`. Exercises `self_cross` blocking `ProjectedFill` while the opposite-side crossing order is open, followed by `CancelOppositeCrossOrder` lifting suppression and settling exact inventory skew.
3. **Invariants**: `TypeOK`, `MonotonicWatermarks`, `ZeroDoubleCountInventorySkew`, `SettledLedgerUnpollutedByProjection`, `StaleStatusIsNoOp`, `SelfCrossSuppressesProjection`, `ExactIncrementalGapPriceDenominator`.
