# Review notes

1. **Telescoping zero-double-count telescoping sum**: Defining `effective_before = max(confirmed_filled, projected_filled)` and emitting `effective_delta = max(0, effective_after - effective_before)` on both `on_projected_fill` and `on_fill_fixed` (`crates/tickwire-strategy-sdk/src/microstructure.rs:818-832, 1034-1058`) guarantees by telescoping induction that cumulative strategy inventory skew equals `max(confirmed_filled, projected_filled)` at every step and converges to exact `settled_filled` once deduplicated `FillEvent`s arrive, regardless of status/fill reordering, drops, or duplicate `trade_id` frames.
2. **Projection isolation from canonical durability**: `Order.{exch_cum_qty, exch_quote_qty, exch_avg_qty, projected_cum_qty}` are strictly transient runtime watermarks excluded from `Order::eq`, `CanonicalRecord`, `AssetView`, and `Position` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/lib.rs:365-430`), so `Account::recover` replays identical settled balances, holds, and positions regardless of whether `projected_fill_enabled` was toggled live.
3. **Separate `exch_quote_qty` and `exch_avg_qty` watermarks**: Because `LimitFallback` status frames advance `exch_cum_qty` without carrying `cumulative_quote` or `avg_px`, incremental notional subtraction must track the exact `cum_qty` at which `prev_cum_quote` (`exch_quote_qty`) or `prev_avg_px` (`exch_avg_qty`) was last recorded.
