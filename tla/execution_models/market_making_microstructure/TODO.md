# Market-making microstructure follow-up

All five Stage 4D cross-layer findings documented in [`L0_stp_modify/04_counterexamples.md`](L0_stp_modify/04_counterexamples.md) and [`L1_projected_fill/04_counterexamples.md`](L1_projected_fill/04_counterexamples.md) have been resolved and covered by regression tests across `tickwire-account`, `tickwire-account-gateway`, `tickwire-strategy-sdk`, `tickwire-binance-account`, and `tickwire-spot-execution`:

1. **Resolved (Bug 1)**: `Order` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/projected_fill.rs`) and `OrderWatermarkEntry` (`crates/tickwire-strategy-sdk/src/microstructure.rs`) track separate `exch_quote_qty` and `exch_avg_qty` denominators alongside `exch_cum_qty`, preventing intermediate `LimitFallback` status frames or settled-first `FillEvent`s from corrupting incremental `CumulativeQuote` / `AveragePrice` gap prices.
2. **Resolved (Bug 2)**: `Account::supersede_pending_modify_on_terminal` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/modifications.rs`) clears in-flight `PendingModify`, releases `shadow_holds` and tentative `new_client_id` indices when a resting order closes via fill or cancel, marks `ModificationRecord.superseded` for bounded admission eviction once the parent order expires, tolerates late `ModifyRejected`, and reopens the order cleanly if an out-of-order Spot `cancelReplace` replacement leg arrives with a brand-new `venue_order_id`.
3. **Resolved (Bug 3)**: `Account::{known_order_quantity, authorized_quantity_bound, authorized_price_bound}` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/amendments.rs` and `crates/tickwire-account/src/events.rs`) include in-flight `PendingModify` and non-aborted/non-rejected `ModificationRecord` terms (`prior_price` and `requested_price` / `requested_quantity`) so out-of-order fills and status frames racing with modifications never falsely quarantine or freeze `Account`.
4. **Resolved (Bug 4)**: `QueryDesk` (`crates/tickwire-account/src/query_desk.rs`) and Binance `AccountSession` (`crates/tickwire-binance-account/src/session.rs`) probe `origClientOrderId = new_client_id` first on `ProductKind::Spot` `AmendAmbiguous` modifications and fall back to `orderId` / `orig_client_id` on `-2013`, while binding rotated Spot `cancelReplace` private `executionReport` frames and historical-leg aliases cleanly across live WS and REST snapshots.
5. **Resolved (Bug 5)**: `AccountSession::{submit_new_order, submit_modify_order}` (`crates/tickwire-spot-execution/src/session.rs`), `AccountSession::submit_modify` (`crates/tickwire-binance-account/src/session.rs`), and `AccountGateway::abort` (`crates/tickwire-account-gateway/src/lib.rs`) enforce local STP and pending-action admission before spending `RateLimiter` command quota and release zero-byte aborts cleanly without retaining active order slots.
