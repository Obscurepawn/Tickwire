# Symbolic `QF_LIA` Linearization & Inductive Drift-Cone Notes — `L1_SymCalibration`

## 1. Critical `QF_NIA` $\to$ `QF_LIA` Linearization Lesson
- In our initial symbolic step check (`IndInv /\ Next => IndInv'`), `Init => IndInv` (`4.45s`) and `IndInv => SafetyConjunction` (`4.53s`) discharged immediately, and 12 of the 15 `Next` transitions discharged in `< 1.5s`, but `Sign`, `CheckSend`, and `CheckForeign` slowed down sharply in Z3.
- **Root cause**: Multiplying or dividing a symbolic integer (`dt_ns`, `mid`, `st_ts`) by an `IF`/`CASE` expression over a symbolic state variable (`dt_ns * (PPB - DriftPpbOf(cfg_drift_case))`, `mid \div UnitNanos(unit)`, `st_ts * UnitNanos(st_unit)`) produces `(* sym_x (ite ...))` or `(div sym_x (ite ...))`, pushing Z3 from linear integer arithmetic (`QF_LIA`) into nonlinear integer arithmetic (`QF_NIA`).
- **Fix (`02_models/L1_SymCalibration.tla:218-280`)**: Distributing multiplication, Euclidean division, and modulo *inside* each `IF`/`CASE` branch (`MulPpb`, `MulMsNs`, `ToCaptureMs`, `DivByUnit`, `MulByUnit`, `AdvanceTruthPs`, `WindowPrecisionOk`) ensures every `*`, `\div`, and `%` has a literal integer constant (`< 2^31`) as its multiplier or divisor. With pure `QF_LIA`, Z3 proves all 151 inductive VCs across all 15 `Next` transitions in `38.501s` (`38.944s` parallel wall time for base + step + entailment combined).
