# State and concurrency

The market owner serializes publisher changes, snapshot construction and JOIN handling. SHM publishes a contiguous batch with one release operation. Each subscriber independently applies at most its poll budget, which can stop between the quote and its FeedState. The model stores the owner's batch history. Each reader derives slots by selecting the most recent record of each kind through its own cursor. Fields from its accepted snapshot get member-local sequence numbers 1 and 2. Later rows retain their ring sequence. A single instrument makes the instrument and topic frontiers equal.

This transcription keeps the live/stale record ordering, publisher and ingress timestamps, lag between cursors, initial JOIN announcement and the execution sink's return value. Quote volume is positive in both the accepted source and our live quote abstraction. Equal timestamps across successive batches are permitted. A stale quote retains its previous price's ingress time. The account and SDK sample monotonic time independently via interleaved steps in one time domain. The SDK callback is represented by its last emitted sequence and decision time. The account's policy bit becomes false after a genuine gap. Membership failure denotes the sticky flag in the application collection, which prevents polling and future transition lookup.

An admitted live JOIN requires a complete public snapshot, while packet arrival order and catch-up before activation do not cause SDK callbacks. The run takes a permitted immediate activation with an empty catch-up. Separate depth getters in Rust check coverage on both sides; on a quote-only topic the publisher has no depth context. This model exercises only that branch, not a Binance depth book. Session leases, malformed inputs, source epoch changes, reconnect orchestration, other instruments and strategies that are temporarily unavailable are outside the explored states. No fairness assumption is needed for the safety properties.
