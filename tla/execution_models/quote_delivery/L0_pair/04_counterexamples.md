# A valid unavailable BBO permanently fails its healthy SHM subscription

The five-state trace starts with a live two-record BBO. The account joins at ring sequence 2. The owner publishes one unavailable BBO as FeedState 3 followed by stale quote 4. The account accepts mark 3 and invalidates the generation. It attempts a second mark at 4 because the feed remains noncontinuous. Policy rejects a newer same-generation mark after invalidation. The SHM source interprets that refusal as a sink failure. The application sets its member's terminal failure bit. [TLC trace](evidence/retention.log)

All frames are valid. The control connection stays open and the actor runs sequentially. Ring generation and source connectivity are separate identities. Same-generation order refusal is the documented safety policy. The additional effect is that future polls skip the member and even an approved successor lookup excludes it at `crates/tickwire-app/src/market.rs:135-160,206-245`. Other members and the account process continue running, but the collection requires all members healthy for new risk. This witness concerns the general Sources object. A single-host spot runner has no public hot-switch command in this profile.

A fix can propagate idempotent unavailability separately from a true subscription/sink error. Keep first-write permission closed for this generation and retain the subscription in a condition where an approved successor can be attached. Regress with a real JOIN, a previously live quote, one unavailable pair and a subsequently approved new generation. Assert that the original generation stays denied and no transport failure is recorded. [Independent review](06_review.md)
