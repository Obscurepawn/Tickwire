# Checked quote and subscription properties

Within one instrument and up to three batches, the SDK predicate recognizes exactly the tail of live pairs applied after JOIN. The account additionally recognizes an authenticated live JOIN cut. The accepted mark always denotes the original ring tail while its permission is live. When the source cursor is between halves, the current market gate rejects risk. Publication, arrival, and sampling may share a timestamp, and expiration may occur between the two reads.

The source and the account policy interact differently after a genuine stale transition. A rejected same-generation mark terminates the member subscription. [Counterexample](04_counterexamples.md) · [Review](06_review.md)

The positive checks exercised all seven action entries reported by TLC across the two configs, including initialization. Key anchors cover the publisher ordering, snapshot ordinal conversion, both consumer checks, per-record sink call and error propagation. The model fixes one live data source and a valid lease. It tests the quote-only branch. It does not verify the internal SHM memory model, depth updates, restart persistence, multi-instrument scheduling or elapsed wall-clock latency.
