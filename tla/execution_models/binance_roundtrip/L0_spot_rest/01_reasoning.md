# Composition and bounds

The node owns the order connection, private session, account WAL and shared quota. Its turns poll clock, market, private feed, account transport/reports and strategy intake in that order. The eligible quote producer and the worker can progress between these actions. A pending external disconnect can still be unknown to the node when it writes. The policy checks the locally observed generation. A gap observed in the private phase can close the first-write gate in the account phase of that very turn.

The initial state represents an approved, reconciled account and a started worker. It requires a usable signed clock sample, a worker command and the account subscriber's mark before admission. Commit success covers registration, signing, closure, account admission WAL and queuing under the one account owner's call. A producing worker's exact input and signing key validation are represented by fixed valid identities. The final send rechecks the time window, the current private and market grants, and the command quota. Time may also expire after the quota debit and before any TLS acceptance. Consequently the sum of writes and remaining credits may be less than the initial credit.

The modeled new limit order spends one normalized cash unit for one normalized lot with zero fee, corresponding to the cash ratio in the spot-fill fixture. Once the native request is accepted, the REST ACK and a matching authenticated execution report can arrive on separate channels in either order. A second delivery presents the same trade identity and economic data at another input-WAL position. The account reducer can apply each input before publishing its history to the host and worker. A worker-visible report in this model means the worker consumed the relevant fill record, not merely that the REST request succeeded.

The model explores normal reporting, duplicate fills, quota denial, a local observation of private disconnect, and expiry before or during the first-write check. Atomic successful admission, one short complete TLS write, a finite number of private deliveries and a fixed catalog/trade price bound the graph. The model does not establish progress under delayed network I/O, startup reconciliation, the exact interval of exchange-side receipt, execution after partial TLS output, report ring overflow, disk errors, process restarts or fee closure.
