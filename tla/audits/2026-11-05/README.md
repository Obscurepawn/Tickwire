# Single-host trading verification at 9b2ed63

Four reproducible failures affect an account journal, a market subscription, a shared account process and a supervised strategy worker. Each has a TLC counterexample, a Linux reproduction and an independent source review. The contradictory exchange reports in the fee case come from a controlled fixture. Their frequency on a live venue is unknown. No test placed orders at a public exchange.

| Finding | Trigger and consequence | Evidence and repair boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fee closure makes an account journal unreplayable | The private terminal report says 0.001 BTC filled. Two authenticated REST scans agree on a cancellation with zero filled and unchanged cash. The account owner appends the proposed fee closure before the reducer rejects it. A new writer fails on that same record when reopening the journal. | Bybit and OKX loopback processes reproduced the failed reopen. Validate a candidate state before the append and publish it after sync. A previously affected WAL needs a recovery rule of its own. ## Trading routes

**Bybit and OKX cash.** The spot runner shares one cash budget across two strategies and accepts at most one in-flight REST request. The model follows an authenticated quote through independent queue arrival, WAL admission, the final authority sample, the first locally accepted TLS byte, venue observations and accounting. Its four configurations preserve the normalized fee-inclusive cash budget and forbid repeat first writes, including a run with a private disconnect, a late fill and a successful writer restart. In fault-free runs, the progress checks assume sufficient funds, a marketable order, current permissions and deadlines, coherent scans and fair scheduling of the producer, owner, venue and private reader. A single producer's order then fills, the account receives its ACK and reconciles. With two producers, one order can legitimately abort before writing any bytes while the account catches up with the other order's fill. An ACK reaches the account-side callback. The file strategy logs it to stderr. Booking cash requires separate economic evidence. [Spot roundtrip](../../execution_models/spot_roundtrip/L0_live_account/05_output.md) · [Fairness assumptions and trace](../../execution_models/spot_roundtrip/L0_live_account/04_counterexamples.md)

**Binance spot.** Its runtime polls market data, the private session, the account and strategy intake in a defined order. The model follows that assembly through quota and signing-time checks, WAL admission, local TLS acceptance of a short REST request, a private fill and a strategy report. The admission step assumes binding and signing succeed. The 27-state witness reaches the end of the route. Another witness delivers the fill to the worker before the runtime consumes its REST ACK. Those are reachable schedules; the outage configurations make no promise about when the venue will reply. [Binance roundtrip](../../execution_models/binance_roundtrip/L0_spot_rest/05_output.md) · [Coverage and witnesses](../../execution_models/binance_roundtrip/L0_spot_rest/03_run_log.md)

A ten-state Bybit/OKX witness also shows a background reader observing a gap after the authority sample and before TLS accepts the byte. The valid-at-sample guarantee is precise enough to test and differs from requiring remote state to remain current throughout the write. [Sampling trace](../../execution_models/spot_roundtrip/L0_live_account/04_evidence/SampleBoundary.log)

One account process owns one venue account. The two models represent separate deployment entry points. Simultaneous independent processes, portfolio risk across venues and an atomic two-leg trade are outside both compositions. They use concrete owner boundaries and explicit external inputs. The shared BBO, queue and account models test contracts that the route models take as inputs. No machine-checked refinement relation connects the Rust, the module models and the two compositions. The USD-M execution, funding and liquidation lifecycle still lacks a complete end-to-end model. [Independent review of both trading routes](roundtrip-review.md)

## Run results

All 20 positive configurations in the nine new models exhausted their finite state spaces. Four additional diagnostic configurations produced the four reported counterexamples at depths 9, 5, 8 and 7 respectively. A clean replay of each returned TLC’s invariant-violation exit code 12. The native fixtures reproduced all four on Linux.

Of the 85 configurations already tracked at the source revision, 80 completed. Five reached the 30-minute limit with states still queued: spot and USD-M buffered depth synchronization on Binance, plus the primary, rounding and USD-M clock calibrations. The last progress records are archived with the model and configuration digests. These five remain open. [Per-configuration results](legacy-runs.md) · [Last progress reports](legacy-time-limits.txt)

## Coverage gaps

The [per-crate inventory](inventory.md) enumerates all 55 workspace crates and every old model’s source lock. The audit found direct source references for 21 crates before this work and 39 with the new levels included. Sixteen have no direct reference. These include instrument identity and indexing, native Binance wire schemas, shared-memory system calls, and the account codec. The [open-boundary map](open-boundaries.md) names the next property for each of those 16 crates. A listed crate may contribute just one transition or one producer obligation. The checked state count is a property of one finite model configuration. Adding the counts would give no measure of total system coverage.

* **Existing models and drift.** The previous 25 levels have 24 line locks. Sixteen contain lines that changed or moved; 7,061 of 12,126 recorded line locations differ. The inventory separates relocated intact ranges from changed, missing or ambiguous ones. Eight matching locks still require the original assumptions. The private-cut model had no prior line lock. For example, the old account gateway modeled append-before-apply but its finite alphabet left out the fee record that breaks restart. [Account drift review](account-drift.md) · [Binance and submission semantics](legacy-boundaries.md) · [Historical TLC configurations and completion status](legacy-runs.md)
* **Control and recovery.** The configuration level covers local receipts. A single composition has yet to join the supervisor's process replacement, approved strategy generation, live quota, new market subscription and in-flight orders. The current spot model projects successful replay. It does not prove recovery from an already poisoned journal.
* **Economic and venue scope.** The spot compositions use bounded order size and cash. Remaining work includes multi-currency/rebate rounding across a complete deployment, overlapping historical pagination with concurrent venue changes and the venue-specific futures lifecycle. Authentication and native schema parsing are exercised in Rust and loopback tests. Catalog identity across venues and stable instrument slots across catalog growth need a model that joins market and account activation. [Catalog](../../../specs/implementation/instrument-catalog.md) · [Instrument index](../../../specs/implementation/instrument-index.md)
* **Machine boundaries.** Abstract ring ordering and WAL sync have older finite models. Their proofs do not derive Linux memory ordering, file-system durability or the TLS implementation from an instruction-level model. Wire-codec equality, SBE decoding, numerical overflow and precision need byte-level or arithmetic-specific verification.
