# Independent review of the two spot trading routes

At `9b2ed63` the two models follow different production entry points, each for one venue account. Both compose successful admission, durable evidence and transport with a finite exchange environment. Successful signing and identity binding are inputs to those steps. Neither composition models an atomic trade across venues.

**Bybit and OKX cash.** One published quote enables an external producer to enqueue a canonical descriptor and the account to observe its own market input. The single owner admits one outstanding request, samples authority, attempts the first local TLS byte and routes the REST result using its pending request slot. Private capture and reconciliation can book a trade independently of the ACK. The ACK endpoint in this model is the account-side strategy callback. In the concrete file-queue implementation that callback logs the reply. There is no modeled file-producer consumption of a reply or a fill. Cash conservation counts whole, fee-inclusive reservation units. A scan activation and a restarted writer each project a successful outcome. The two temporal checks disable gaps, deadline expiry and restart. They assume a fresh quote, sufficient funds, stable successful scans and weak fairness for the named producer, owner and venue actions, including acceptance, matching, capture and drain. With one member TLC checks eventual ACK, booking and restored permission. With two it checks settlement and allows a zero-byte abort. The fault configurations check safety. A background reader may observe a gap after the local authority sample and before byte acceptance. **Binance spot REST.** One qualified hosted worker consumes a depth trigger. The runtime polls clock, market, private input, account and strategy intake in source order. The model represents a successful signature and registration at admission, a local deadline and quota check, and one short request accepted in full by local TLS. A separate private input advances the account journal and economic state, and the host passes the resulting record to the worker. A second delivery of the modeled trade advances the input cursor while retaining one debit. The 27-state and 22-state traces establish reachability only. The shorter trace shows worker delivery before the runtime consumes the REST ACK. It establishes no ordering between the venue's original transmissions. Both configurations allow indefinite stuttering.
