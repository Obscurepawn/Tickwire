# Closure of the four trading-route findings

| Finding | Observable behavior after the fix | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terminal and fee evidence | A rejected fee closure leaves the WAL, history frontier and ledger sequence unchanged. Replay of the historical contradictory record keeps the order's fees open and the account frozen. Once authentic fill and wallet evidence agree, recovery can complete. | [Fee-closure model](../../execution_models/cash_evidence/L1_fee_closure/05_output.md) · [Gateway regression](../../../crates/tickwire-account-gateway/tests/lifecycle.rs) · [Legacy recovery cases](../../../crates/tickwire-account/tests/recovery.rs) |
| Multi-strategy order management | An authenticated member attempting to manage another member's known order receives `ManagementDenied`. On the same account process the true owner's next cancel reaches the exchange and gets its own ACK. | [Management-refusal model](../../execution_models/strategy_intake/L1_management_refusal/05_output.md) · [Bybit and OKX live-process regressions](../../execution_models/strategy_intake/L1_management_refusal/evidence/native/result.log) |
| Unavailable market quote | The account consumer advances through both stale records and the next complete quote. The invalidated generation stays ineligible to trade. A subsequent authorized generation can supply a fresh mark. | [Quote-delivery model](../../execution_models/quote_delivery/L0_pair_v2/05_output.md) · [Linux publisher and JOIN test](../../../crates/tickwire-spot-runner/tests/reconcile_cli/market.rs) |
| Hosted worker receipt time | The host timestamps each received control packet after the socket read. A valid receipt created during the poll advances the transition. A future timestamp or an expired approval still fails validation. | [Receipt-time model](../../execution_models/worker_handover/L1_receipt_time/05_output.md) · [Authenticated socket test](../../../crates/tickwire-strategy-host/src/market_transition/tests.rs) |
