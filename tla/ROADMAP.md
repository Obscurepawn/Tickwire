# TLA+ Model Family Roadmap

Each directory under `tla/execution_models/` links its TLA+ specifications and finite `.cfg` bounds to concrete Rust source anchors, modeling assumptions, and recorded TLC outputs. Run any individual family from the repository root with `./scripts/check-tla.sh <family>` (for example, `./scripts/check-tla.sh account_gateway`).

## Verified Subsystem Boundaries

| Subsystem Boundary | Model Families & Scope |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Order Intake & Submission** | [Submission](execution_models/submission/ROADMAP.md) models local commit receipts, lost receipts, and frozen restarts. [Ordered Abort](execution_models/mock_abort/ROADMAP.md) verifies zero-byte unsent abort ordering and replay against the mock execution journal. |
| **Account State & First-Byte Gate** | [Account](execution_models/account/ROADMAP.md) checks multi-asset holds, late fills, reduce-only limits, and fee closure. [Account Gateway](execution_models/account_gateway/ROADMAP.md) composes the account reducer, local WAL, and synchronous authorization at the first TLS byte. |
| **Durable Journals & Closures** | [Physical Journal](execution_models/physical_journal/ROADMAP.md) models batch `fsync` and torn-tail truncation. [Business Journal](execution_models/business_journal/ROADMAP.md), [Account Journal](execution_models/account_journal/ROADMAP.md), and [Recovery Closure](execution_models/recovery_closure/ROADMAP.md) verify hash-chained records, atomic rename + directory sync durability, and dependency verification. |
| **Guarded Network Transport** | [Gateway](execution_models/gateway/ROADMAP.md) traces durable admissions into outbound sessions. [Transport](execution_models/transport/ROADMAP.md) models the exact transition from `0` accepted bytes (`NoBytes`) to `MayHaveSent`, partial writes, and TLS buffer flush. |
| **Hot-Path Decoupling & Retention** | [Hot-Path Decoupling & Windowed Pools](execution_models/hotpath_decoupling/ROADMAP.md) verifies off-hot-path `DialBaton` + `IpPool` handoffs, `AsyncRestQuerier` reply-slot reservations and counted-write retry bounds, active-protected `WindowedTable` eviction, isolated management headroom, and `AccountJournal` prefix compaction. |
| **Per-Order Doubt Isolation & `QueryDesk`** | [QueryDesk & Single-Order Doubt Isolation](execution_models/query_desk/ROADMAP.md) verifies locked cash/`shadow_holds`/`reduce` reservations under per-order doubt, healthy sibling liveness across isolated socket drops and deterministic `VenueEventBody::Rejected`, grace-gated `CloseUncreated`, contradicted-`uncreated` reopening, `Place` + `Cancel` + `Trades` coalescing, Bybit `/v5/order/realtime -> /v5/order/history` chaining, zero-byte `on_not_sent` accounting, and concurrent private WebSocket convergence. |
| **Multi-Connection Order Router & Multi-IP Quota** | [Connection Router & Venue Rate Limiter](execution_models/connection_router_quota/ROADMAP.md) verifies `SendSlot::Exclusive` (`<= 1`) and `SendSlot::Shared` (`<= MaxShared`) concurrency bounds, integer EWMA warmup round-robin and `9/8` hysteresis, epoch-fenced inflight accounting, pre-EOF `retained_receive` / `draining_eof` draining before `DialBaton` reconnect, `FixedWindow` and coalescing `RollingWindow` caps, reverse-order `put_back` rollback, universal `can_afford` == `allow` agreement across duplicate `RateLimitId` spends, action-class isolation, and per-IP `429` failover vs. `418` account freeze. |
| **Market-Making Microstructure (`StpBook`, `Modify`, `ProjectedFill`)** | [Market-Making Microstructure](execution_models/market_making_microstructure/ROADMAP.md) verifies lock-free atomic `best_bid_ticks` / `best_ask_ticks` across dual-price `aggressive_ticks` and `Vec::swap_remove`, single-in-flight `PendingModify` gating, worst-case Spot principal and Futures `shadow_holds` solvency across venue clamping, terminal `PendingModify` superseding, two-phase Spot `new_client_id -> orderId` `AmendAmbiguous` query resolution, pre-admission local refusal non-burn, monotonic `settled <= projected` watermarks, `CHECK_STP` `self_cross` projection suppression, exact `exch_quote_qty` gap-price denominators, and zero-double-count `FillWatermarkTracker` inventory skew. |
| **Ultra-Low-Latency Hot-Path Optimizations** | [Ultra-Low-Latency Hot-Path Optimizations](execution_models/hotpath_ultralow_latency/ROADMAP.md) verifies lifetime-pinned `&MarketRecord` / `MarketStateRef` zero-copy SHM borrows (`ZeroNoAliasWhileBorrowed`, `ZeroCopyLayoutAndHeaderSoundness`), compile-time `crc32` gating across IPC framing and physical WAL SHA-256 chain recovery (`CrcGateModeConsistency`, `WalRecoverySoundWithoutCrc`), side-effect-free hot-path cache warming and isolated loopback `TlsLoopbackWarmer` AEAD nonce synchronization (`LiveBusinessStateInvariance`, `LiveTlsNonceSynchronization`, `DummyLoopbackTlsPairSynchronization`, `WarmupStrictlyRespectsIdlePolicy`), audited SIMD JSON spot BBO fast-path equivalence against the `serde_json` oracle (`ExactOracleEquivalence`, `TickLotAndPositiveSpreadSoundness`, `ZeroHotPathHeapAllocation`), and workspace-wide `overflow-checks = true` trapping across all Cargo profiles (`NoSilentArithmeticWrap`). |
| **Market Data & Venue Recovery** | [Order Book](execution_models/book_state/ROADMAP.md) checks sequence continuity and partial snapshot coverage. [Binance Depth Sync](execution_models/binance_depth_sync/ROADMAP.md) models WebSocket delta buffering across HTTPS snapshots and epochs. [Binance Private Session](execution_models/binance_private_session/ROADMAP.md) tracks user-stream gaps, and [Spot Account Recovery](execution_models/spot_account_recovery/README.md) checks Bybit/OKX dual REST scans against private WAL cuts. |
| **Shared Memory & Local Control** | [Broadcast Ring](execution_models/broadcast_ring/ROADMAP.md) verifies SPMC batch claims, `RecordBorrow` guards, wraparound, and bank freeze under acquire/release semantics. [JOIN Owner](execution_models/join_owner/ROADMAP.md) models late-reader catch-up and unforgeable detachment, and [Control Requests](execution_models/control_requests/ROADMAP.md) covers authenticated sessions and idempotent request stores. |
| **Monotonic Time & Calibration** | [Clock](execution_models/clock/ROADMAP.md) and [System Clock](execution_models/system_clock/ROADMAP.md) verify server-time interval extrapolation under drift bounds and permanent fault latching on backward readings. |
